# Store badges

These are the official, unmodified badge files. Use them as they are: don't
recolour them, stretch them or change their wording.

| File              | Source |
|-------------------|--------|
| `app-store.svg`   | Apple, "Download on the App Store" (US-UK, black): `https://developer.apple.com/assets/elements/badges/download-on-the-app-store.svg` (from https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/) |
| `google-play.svg` | Google, "Get it on Google Play" (English, current design). Google's badge page (https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/) was unreachable from the build environment, so this copy comes from the npm package `@rewilok/app-store-badges@0.1.3` (`assets/google_play_badge/black/en.svg`), which redistributes the official artwork. Replace it with a fresh download from Google's page whenever convenient. |

Apple, the Apple logo, and App Store are trademarks of Apple Inc., registered
in the U.S. and other countries and regions. Google Play and the Google Play
logo are trademarks of Google LLC.

Brand rules worth remembering: link each badge to the app's own store listing,
show both badges at the same height, and keep clear space around them.
